Shogi split assets pack
=======================

Contents
- backgrounds/: background image
- board/: empty 9x9 shogi board
- pieces/normal/: 9 normal piece PNGs split individually
- pieces/promoted/: 6 promoted piece PNGs split individually (red text)
- ui/: timer panel, piece tray, nameplate, and split highlights
- digits/: timer digits 0-9 and colon split individually
- sheets/: original combined sheets

Normal piece mapping
ou, gyoku, hisha, kaku, kin, gin, kei, kyo, fu

Promoted piece mapping
to, narikyo, narikei, narigin, uma, ryu
